# Pin Map — ESP32-C6-DevKitM-1 on the SaltMon Carrier

Source of truth for firmware (`firmware/main/board.h`) and the carrier schematic. Header pinout from
`esp32-c6-devkitm-1-schematics.pdf`. Symbol/footprint: `kicad/lib/SaltMon` (`ESP32-C6-DevKitM-1`,
pads 1–15 = J1-1…15, 16–30 = J3-1…15).

Carrier reference designators (schematic `kicad/DepthSensor/DepthSensor.kicad_sch`, generated by
`gen_schematic.py`): **J1** DC jack, **J2** J_SNS, **J3** J_PANEL, **J4** J_SPARE, **U1** kit, **U2** TBD62783APG,
**RN1** 8× 100 kΩ bussed pull-down network, R1–R8 LED, R9 power LED, R10 button pull-up, R11/R12 sensor series.
"Kit pin J1-n / J3-n" below means the kit's own headers, not the carrier's J1/J3.

## Carrier Connectors

### J_SNS — 2×3 shrouded, to US-100 (ribbon ~1.2 m)

| Pin | Net | US-100 pin (Dupont 1×5) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `+3V3` | VCC |
| 2 | `SNS_TX_C` (from GPIO22 via 100 Ω) | Echo/RX |
| 3 | `SNS_RX_C` (to GPIO23 via 100 Ω) | Trig/TX |
| 4 | `GND` | GND |
| 5 | `GND` | GND |
| 6 | — | not connected |

The US-100 UART-mode jumper must be installed.

### J_PANEL — 2×8 shrouded, to hand-wired lid (ribbon ~20 cm)

| Pin | Net | Lid part | | Pin | Net | Lid part |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `LED_A1` | Green anode | | 2 | `LED_A2` | Yellow anode |
| 3 | `LED_A3` | Orange anode | | 4 | `LED_A4` | Red anode |
| 5 | `LED_A5` | Blue anode | | 6 | `LED_A6` | Purple anode |
| 7 | `LED_A7` | Bi-color green anode | | 8 | `LED_A8` | Bi-color red anode |
| 9 | `LED_PWR` | White anode | | 10 | `BTN_N` | Push-button |
| 11 | `SW_A` | Switch common | | 12 | `SW_A` | Switch common |
| 13 | `SW_B` | Switch throw | | 14 | `SW_B` | Switch throw |
| 15 | `GND` | LED cathodes, button | | 16 | `GND` | LED cathodes, button |

`LED_An` = U2 OUTn → current resistor on the carrier (values in requirements §5.1).

### J_SPARE — 1×4

| 2 | `GND` |

| 4 | `SPARE_B` (GPIO21) |

## U2 TBD62783APG (DIP-18)

| U2 pin | Signal | U2 pin | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 IN1 | `LED_CH1` | 18 OUT1 | → R1 → `LED_A1` |
| 2 IN2 | `LED_CH2` | 17 OUT2 | → R2 → `LED_A2` |
| 3 IN3 | `LED_CH3` | 16 OUT3 | → R3 → `LED_A3` |
| 4 IN4 | `LED_CH4` | 15 OUT4 | → R4 → `LED_A4` |
| 5 IN5 | `LED_CH5` | 14 OUT5 | → R5 → `LED_A5` |
| 6 IN6 | `LED_CH6` | 13 OUT6 | → R6 → `LED_A6` |
| 7 IN7 | `LED_CH7` | 12 OUT7 | → R7 → `LED_A7` |
| 8 IN8 | `LED_CH8` | 11 OUT8 | → R8 → `LED_A8` |
| 9 VCC | `V_SYS` | 10 GND | `GND` |

Each IN has a 100 kΩ pull-down to GND.

Pin assignment checked against the KiCad `Transistor_Array:TBD62783A` symbol (pin 9 VCC, pin 10 GND).
