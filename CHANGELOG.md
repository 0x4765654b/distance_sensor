# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Hardware revisions are named after the carrier PCB
revision.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- README and CHANGELOG.
- **Lid panel PCB rev 0.1** (`kicad/LidPanel`), replacing the hand-wired lid (requirements v0.8: D-10, §3.2, §5.3,
  MEC-06, MEC-09). It is 50 × 63 mm and passive: 8 flat-top 5 mm LEDs, J2/J3 pads for the panel switch and button,
  and a right-angle 2×8 header J_LID wired 1:1 to J_PANEL. ERC and DRC clean, schematic parity clean.
  - The 1:1 lid drilling template is on the board's User.Drawings layer.
  - The lid ribbon now has an IDC socket at both ends. Bezels, the GND bus wire and most heat-shrink are gone.
  - `docs/lid-pick-list.md`: the lid board's own pick list, with 10 mm nylon LED spacers as the LED housings, and an
    assembly order.
- `kicad/lib/schgen.py` and `kicad/lib/pcbgen.py`: generator helpers shared by both boards. The carrier's
  generators now use them, with unchanged output.

### Changed
- **Carrier rev 0.2:** removed the top tab, which held no parts. The board is now 80.5 × 92 mm, with only the
  bottom tab (the kit's USB end and J1 must reach the bottom wall, MEC-03). The silkscreen reads v0.2.
  - Rerouted. C4 and J4 moved 0.5 mm down, and a fixed +3V3 stub leads U1 pad 1 out from under the antenna slot
    (`PREROUTE` in `gen_pcb.py`).
  - DRC: 0 errors, 0 unconnected items, schematic parity clean.
  - The Gerbers in `depth_gerber/` are still rev 0.1 and must be re-exported.

## [carrier-0.1] — 2026-09-28

First complete carrier design, ready for fabrication.

### Added
- **Requirements** (`docs/requirements.md`, draft v0.7): a design-decisions log, components, the power path and
  budget, LED behavior, sensor and MCU I/O, firmware, the two-stack cloud design (SaltMon + ChoreCore consumer),
  enclosure mechanics, and a verification plan.
- **Pin map** (`docs/pinmap.md`): the kit header pads, J_SNS, J_PANEL, J_SPARE and the U2 pinout.
- **Integration contract** (`docs/integration-contract.md`, contract v1): SSM discovery, the SNS/SQS subscription,
  the message envelope, the payload for each kind, delivery semantics and the evolution rules.
- **Pick list** (`docs/pick-list.md`) for one complete build.
- **KiCad library** (`kicad/lib/`): an ESP32-C6-DevKitM-1 symbol and a socketed footprint with the antenna
  keep-out, generated by `gen_devkit.py`.
- **Carrier schematic**, generated by `gen_schematic.py`. ERC is clean. The UUIDs are deterministic, so
  regenerating keeps the links to the PCB.
- **Carrier PCB**, generated by `gen_pcb.py`:
  - 80.5 × 106 mm board with four notched corners, an antenna slot and cable-tie holes;
  - placement, autorouting with Freerouting 2.4.1, and a GND pour on both layers;
  - power net classes: 0.4–0.8 mm tracks.
  - DRC: 0 errors, 0 unconnected items, schematic parity clean.
- Gerbers and drill files (`kicad/DepthSensor/depth_gerber/`).

### Changed
- MEC-07: the box is mounted through its 4 corner holes. The wall-screw keep-clear zones on the carrier were
  removed.
- Test points and mounting holes are excluded from the BOM.

### Known Issues
- DRC warning: the J1 (PJ-102AH) footprint differs from the library copy, because its silkscreen is trimmed at
  the board edge. Expected.
